## Basic Definition


[ISP](https://www.lokiproxy.com/?utm_t=1&utm_i=52) stands for Internet Service Provider. In simple terms, it is a service provider that offers internet access to individuals and businesses. The home broadband and enterprise dedicated networks we use daily are all provided by ISP operators. Their core function is to establish a connection bridge between terminal devices and the internet backbone network, serving as the underlying infrastructure for all network access.


In industry terminology, ISP IP refers to public network addresses assigned by legitimate operators, which come with complete carrier network traceability attributes.


## Core Characteristics


IP resources assigned by legitimate ISPs come with complete carrier registration information, clear ownership traceability, and standardized network attributes. Compared to traditional data center IPs, these resources typically have higher purity and are less likely to suffer from collateral issues caused by multiple users sharing the same IP segment, resulting in lower probabilities of network jitter and access restrictions.


The stable operating environment and standardized network labeling give ISP-type IPs better platform recognition, business trustworthiness, and system compatibility in compliant commercial scenarios, effectively reducing various access anomalies that could otherwise disrupt project progress.
