## Core Differences Among the Three Proxy Types


Different proxy types serve different purposes, and the key is whether they fit your specific business needs. Enterprises should evaluate their business characteristics thoroughly and treat proxy resources as a flexible technical tool that can be adapted accordingly.


### [Rotating Proxy](https://www.lokiproxy.com/?utm_t=1&utm_i=52)

Rotating proxies employ an automatic rotation mechanism, assigning a different IP address for each connection request. Their core advantage lies in load distribution capabilities in high-concurrency scenarios. When businesses need to conduct large-scale market price collection, search engine result analysis, or multi-region brand presence monitoring, frequent requests from a single address often fail to meet efficiency requirements. A rotating proxy pool, backed by a vast pool of address resources, can effectively distribute request pressure.


### [Static Proxy](https://www.lokiproxy.com/?utm_t=1&utm_i=52)

Static proxies assign a long-term, fixed network address to users. Their core value lies in the continuity of network identity. When managing e-commerce platform backend data, maintaining daily operations of corporate social media accounts, or conducting long-term website availability monitoring, a fixed network identity helps reduce verification costs associated with frequent address changes.


### [Mobile Proxy](https://www.lokiproxy.com/?utm_t=1&utm_i=52)

Mobile proxies rely on cellular mobile communication network resources, typically originating from real mobile devices such as smartphones and tablets, offering a high level of trustworthiness. Such proxies offer distinct advantages in scenarios like advertising performance testing or location-based service verification.


## Selection Strategy: Match by Use Case

### Scenarios Suited for Rotating Proxies

For business logic involving short-connection, high-frequency one-time data retrieval that does not depend on session persistence across requests, rotating proxies can meet the requirements with lower management overhead.


### Scenarios Suited for Static Proxies

For businesses that require maintaining a long-term consistent network identity, such as account session persistence, continuous data tracking, or scenarios that rely on local cache retention.


### Scenarios Suited for Mobile Proxies

For businesses primarily focused on mobile app-side operations, where the target platform has strict risk control measures, and tasks that do not require a persistent fixed IP identity.
